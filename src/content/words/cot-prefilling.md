word: "cot prefilling"
meaning: "A mechanism where we tell an AI model to think in an exact way by mandating its chain of thought (cot) to have a specific exact pattern of words. For example, if we tell the model: hey AI output like this - <think>I must first see... Then I must see..Finally my anser...<think>, then we are doing cot prefilling. We basically override the model's own thinking pattern."
