---
word: ephemeral
pronunciation: /ɪˈfɛm(ə)r(ə)l/
partOfSpeech: adjective
meaning: Lasting a very short time.
examples:
  - "Attention online is ephemeral — a post is old in six hours."
  - "The mayfly's whole adult life is ephemeral: one day, sometimes less."
etymology: From Greek *ephēmeros* — "lasting only a day" (*epi*, upon + *hēmera*, day).
tags: [adjective, time]
added: 2026-08-20
