---
word: hiraeth
pronunciation: /ˈhɪərʌɪθ/
partOfSpeech: noun
meaning: A homesickness for a home you cannot return to, or that never existed.
examples:
  - "Reading about his grandparents' village gave him a hiraeth for a place he'd never seen."
etymology: Welsh. Usually said to be untranslatable — the standard move when a word carries more grief than its dictionary line.
tags: [noun, feeling, untranslatable, favourite]
added: 2026-08-27
---

Sits close to Portuguese *saudade* and German *Sehnsucht*. Each language seems to need its own version.
