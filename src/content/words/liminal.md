---
word: liminal
pronunciation: /ˈlɪmɪn(ə)l/
partOfSpeech: adjective
meaning: Occupying a threshold — in between one state and the next, belonging fully to neither.
examples:
  - "Airports at 3 a.m. are liminal spaces: everyone is going somewhere, nobody is anywhere."
  - "The week between resigning and starting the new job felt liminal."
etymology: From Latin *limen*, a doorway or threshold.
tags: [adjective, space, time, favourite]
added: 2026-08-16
---

Useful precisely because English has few good words for *the middle of a transition*. Related to [[petrichor]] only in that both name something everyone has felt and few can name.
