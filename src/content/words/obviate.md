---
word: obviate
pronunciation: /ˈɒbvɪeɪt/
partOfSpeech: verb
meaning: To remove a difficulty before it arrives — to make it unnecessary rather than solve it.
examples:
  - "Caching the result obviates the second database call entirely."
  - "A clear brief obviates most of the arguments that follow."
etymology: Latin *obviare*, to meet in the way — to head something off.
tags: [verb, work, favourite]
added: 2026-09-02
---

The precision worth keeping: you don't obviate a problem you already have. You obviate one you were about to have.
