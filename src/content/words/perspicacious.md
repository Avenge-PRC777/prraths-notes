---
word: perspicacious
pronunciation: /ˌpəːspɪˈkeɪʃəs/
partOfSpeech: adjective
meaning: Having sharp insight; quick to notice and understand what others miss.
examples:
  - "A perspicacious question that reframed the whole meeting."
etymology: Latin *perspicax*, sharp-sighted — from *perspicere*, to see through.
tags: [adjective, mind]
added: 2026-08-29
---

Not the same as *perspicuous*, which means clearly expressed. One is about the seer, the other about the thing seen.
