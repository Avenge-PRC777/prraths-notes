---
word: sonder
partOfSpeech: noun
meaning: The realisation that every stranger you pass is living a life as vivid and complicated as your own.
examples:
  - "Stuck in traffic, he was struck by sonder — a thousand cars, a thousand entire lives."
etymology: Invented in 2012 for *The Dictionary of Obscure Sorrows*. Not a real historical word, which is part of the point.
tags: [noun, feeling, people]
added: 2026-08-19
---

Worth knowing it's a coinage, not a discovery. But it names something so common that it's been quietly adopted anyway — which is how words get real.
